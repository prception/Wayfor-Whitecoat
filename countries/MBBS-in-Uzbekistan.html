Eligibility Criteria to Apply

To secure MBBS admission in Uzbekistan, students must meet the following criteria:

  • Must include Physics, Chemistry, and Biology.
  • The minimum percentage varies from university to university.

The intake usually happens once a year, and the academic year starts in September. There is winter intake for certain courses, but the main intake will be in September.

Documents Needed:

  • A recent passport-sized photograph.

Pathway Particulars

Duration & Structure

The MBBS program usually lasts for 6 years. This includes a perfect blend of theoretical studies, clinical practice, and internships within this period. The first couple of years focus on anatomy, physiology, and biochemistry, whereas later years emphasize clinical skills and internships.

Medium of Teaching

Most medical colleges provide MBBS programs in the English medium to international students, eliminating linguistic barriers. Other universities also offer programs in the Russian language, but most international students apply for English-medium courses.

MBBS Admission In Uzbekistan

A step-by-step roadmap to admission

The admission process is very easy and planned such that it may be easily accessible to international students.

  1. Application Submission: Send application forms with personal information, academic qualifications, and a recent photograph.
  2. Eligibility Check: Candidates need a 12th standard pass in the science stream (PCB). Admission can be direct or via entrance exams.
  3. Visa & Payment: Tuition fees must be paid on arrival of the visa according to the fee structure.
  4. Arrival & Orientation: After arriving, attend orientation to familiarize with the campus, culture, and studies.

Career Opportunities After MBBS

With globally recognized degrees, MBBS graduates from Uzbekistan have diverse and rewarding career prospects.

  • Practice in India: Return to India and appear for the FMGE/NExT exam to obtain a medical license.
  • Postgraduate Studies: Pursue PG medical education in India, Uzbekistan, or countries like the USA, UK, Australia, and Canada.
  • Global Exams: Clear licensing exams such as USMLE, PLAB, or AMC.
  • Diverse Roles: Work as general practitioners, junior doctors, researchers, or in public health, healthcare management, and telemedicine.

MBBS in Uzbekistan Syllabus

A perfect blend of theoretical studies, clinical practice, and internships.

  • Year 1-2: Anatomy, Histology, Biophysics, Medical Chemistry, Basic Biology, Physiology, Biochemistry, Microbiology, Pathology, Pharmacology.
  • Year 3-4: General Medicine, General Surgery, Radiology, Immunology, Epidemiology, Internal Medicine, Obstetrics & Gynecology, Pediatrics, Neurology, Dermatology.
  • Year 5: Orthopedics, Ophthalmology, Psychiatry, ENT, Emergency Medicine, Infectious Diseases.
  • Year 6: Clinical Rotations in Medicine, Surgery, Pediatrics, Gynecology, Emergency Care, Specialized Departments.
