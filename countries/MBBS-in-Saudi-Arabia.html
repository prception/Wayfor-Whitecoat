About Saudi Arabia Overview

MBBS in Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia is the largest country in both the Middle East and the Arabian Peninsula, known for its modern cities and its rich cultural heritage, making it economically strong. These factors combined attribute to Saudi Arabia being a global education and healthcare hub.

The nation's focus and investment in education and healthcare infrastructure makes Saudi Arabia home to numerous prestigious medical schools. The international accreditation and cutting-edge training and research opportunities offered makes the MBBS programs highly sought after.

Students are offered a variety of academic resources in a safe, orderly, and culturally rich environment. Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam are home to many of the top medical colleges with modern campuses, advanced laboratories, and distinguished faculty.

All Requirements for MBBS in Saudi Arabia

Understand the essential criteria and requirements

Ensure your eligibility for pursuing MBBS and starting your medical journey.

Eligibility Criteria to Apply

Applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • Completed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology as main subjects.
  • A minimum of 70–90% aggregate score in PCB (varies by university).
  • Valid NEET qualification score (required for Indian students).
  • At least 17 years old at the time of admission.
  • Submit English proficiency scores such as IELTS or TOEFL.
  • Some universities may conduct an entrance exam or interview.

Required Documents & Intake

The annual intake period for studying MBBS is only once a year, commonly occurring around September or October. Students are encouraged to apply at least six months in advance, during April to July.

Documents Needed:

  • 10th and 12th Marksheet & Birth Certificate.
  • Photocopy of Invitation Letter & 5 passport size photographs.
  • Report on COVID test & Bank receipt.
  • Statement of Purpose (attested by a governing body).

Saudi medical schools provide on-campus hostels with modern conveniences (Wi-Fi, AC, laundry) and separate dorms for male and female students. University canteens and nearby eateries offer Indian and Asian cuisines, and many hostels provide cooking facilities.

Traveling is economical; public transport such as Careem, Uber, taxis, and buses are readily available in Riyadh and Jeddah. Average monthly travel costs are between SAR 150 and SAR 300.

Pathway Particulars

Duration & Structure

The course duration spans 6 years of academic study and a 1-year internship, totaling 7 years. Students go through foundational, pre-clinical, clinical, and para-clinical subjects, completing hospital rotations under supervision in the final year.

Medium of Teaching

English is preferred as the primary medium of instruction in Saudi Arabian universities for overseas students, alleviating academic adjustment challenges for Indian students.

MBBS Admission In Saudi Arabia

A step-by-step roadmap to admission

Applying for MBBS becomes highly competitive during the first wave of applications for outstanding medical colleges.

  1. Fill out the admission form: Visit the official website and complete the MBBS admission form.
  2. Verification: The university will verify your application form and uploaded documents.
  3. Screening: The university undertakes a screening process to determine eligibility.
  4. Visa Process: Apply for a student visa to the nearest Embassy or consulate, attaching confirmation of admission.
  5. Enrollment: Receive the physical admission letter, travel to Saudi Arabia, and complete enrollment.

Scholarships for MBBS in Saudi Arabia

Major funders are the Saudi government, universities, and international institutions.

  • Government-Funded: Fully funded scholarships cover full tuition, monthly living allowance, free accommodation, medical insurance, and airfare.
  • University-Specific: Merit-based scholarships awarded by top colleges based on academic performance and entrance scores.
  • IsDB Scholarship: Provided to students from member countries (including India), paying for tuition, living, and post-graduation expenses.

Career Opportunities After MBBS

Medical graduates can apply for residency positions, practice in private or government institutions, or further their studies.

  • Local Practice: The Saudi healthcare sector offers competitive remuneration and well-established infrastructure.
  • Practice in India: International students who return home can appear for licensing examinations like FMGE/NExT to practice in India.
  • International Opportunities: The degree is internationally accepted by the UK, USA, and Gulf countries.
