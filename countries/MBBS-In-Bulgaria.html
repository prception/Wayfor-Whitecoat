About Bulgaria Overview

MBBS in Bulgaria

Bulgaria is located in Central Europe, offering a balanced lifestyle with modern urban cities, well-developed infrastructure, and beautiful natural landscapes. The country homes several well-known universities of global repute and enjoys a stable political environment, making it a reliable destination for pursuing medical education.

The destination is favourable for students residing in India, Pakistan, and Bangladesh. Compared to many Western European nations, the cost of living in Bulgaria is relatively low, making it an ideal choice for high-quality European education at an affordable budget.

The official language of instruction for MBBS programs is English, allowing international students to adapt easily without language barriers. The presence of NMC-recognized and globally accredited medical universities ensures that students receive internationally accepted degrees.

All Requirements for MBBS in Bulgaria

Ensure your eligibility for pursuing MBBS and starting your medical journey.

Eligibility Criteria to Apply

To secure MBBS admission in Bulgaria, students must meet the following criteria:

  • Age should be 17 years by 31st December in the Admission Year.
  • Must have completed 10+2 education with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Minimum 70% to 75%).
  • 10+2 education must have been in an English-medium stream.
  • NEET should be qualified.
  • University Entrance Exam may be required.

Required Documents & Intake

MBBS intakes in Bulgaria usually occur once a year, between September and October. Students should apply 6–8 months in advance.

Documents Needed:

  • 10th & 12th Mark sheet & Passing Certificate.
  • Passport, Aadhaar copy & NEET result.
  • Birth Certificate & Transfer Certificate.
  • PCC, Medical certificate/HIV & 6 Month Bank Statement.
  • 10 Photographs, 2 Recommendation Letters, SOP & CV.

Campus Life & Living Expenses

Cost of Living Ranges between ₹12,000 to ₹20,000 monthly.

Most Bulgarian medical universities provide hostel facilities with furnished rooms, Wi-Fi, and security, costing between ₹10,000 to ₹20,000 per month. Indian food is available in major cities, and many hostels offer Indian mess facilities or options to cook.

Public transport is affordable and well-connected. Students use buses, trams, metros, and bicycles for daily commuting, with monthly transport expenses ranging between ₹3,000 to ₹6,000.

Duration & Structure

The MBBS course duration in Bulgaria is 6 years, including 5 years of academic education and 1 year of compulsory internship.

Medium of Teaching

The medium of teaching for MBBS in Bulgaria is English, eliminating language barriers for international students.

MBBS Admission In Bulgaria

A step-by-step roadmap to admission

The students are not required to clear English Language tests such as IELTS or TOEFL for most universities.

  1. Fill out the admission form: Complete the form on the official website.

  3. Screening: University screening and eligibility check.
