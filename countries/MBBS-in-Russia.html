Eligibility Criteria to Apply

To secure MBBS admission in Russia in 2026, students must meet the following criteria:

  • Age should be 17 years by 31st December in the Admission Year.
  • Must have completed 10+2 education with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Minimum 50% required).
  • NEET should be qualified.
  • University Entrance Exam (Online Mode) may be required.

Required Documents & Intake

Intake usually starts every year in September. The admission process generally starts as early as April and extends up to August.

Documents Needed:

  • 10th Mark sheet & 12th Mark sheet & Passing Certificate.
  • Valid Passport & Aadhar copy.
  • NEET result scorecard.
  • 10 White Background Photographs (Size 3.5 X 4.5 CM).
  • A genuine migration document from the previous educational institute.

Pathway Particulars

Duration & Structure

The course of MBBS in Russia comprises a tenure period of 6 years (5 years study + 1 year internship). The first couple of years are devoted to subjects like anatomy and physiology, while later years focus on clinical training and hands-on experience.

Medium of Teaching

The medium of teaching is primarily English for international students, ensuring language won't act as a barrier. Russian language may be required for clinical interactions.

MBBS Admission In Russia

A step-by-step roadmap to admission

The admission process is fairly easy and hassle-free, and students need not appear in any extra entrance test like NEET.

  1. Fill out the admission form: Complete the admission form from the RMC website.
  2. Select your University: Select your NMC-approved desired university to study MBBS.
  3. Submit Documents: Submit scanned documents: 10th and 12th mark sheets, NEET score card, copy of the medical certificate, and passport.
  4. Receive Invitation: After 10-15 days of applying, you'll get the invitation letter from the university.
  5. Apply for Visa: Students can apply for a visa in the respective embassy for 10 days of visa processing.

Scholarships for MBBS in Russia

Financial constraints do not become significant barriers through scholarships.

  • Government of Russian Federation Scholarships: Covers tuition fees, accommodation costs, and maintenance allowance.
  • Benjamin A. Gilman Scholarships: An educational grant to students from abroad who have a very good academic record.
  • SRAS: Targets applicants in the US. Other non-governmental or university-specific scholarships are open to international students.

Career Opportunities After MBBS

The career prospects can range from highly professional roles locally to international opportunities.

  • Practice in Hospitals: Start work as doctors in hospitals, clinics, or health centers in surgery, pediatrics, cardiology, etc..
  • Advanced Specialization: Pursue advanced specialization through postgraduate studies.
  • International Organizations: Work in organizations such as WHO or UNICEF for global health initiatives.
  • Other Roles: Enter teaching, open private clinics, or go into public health administration.
