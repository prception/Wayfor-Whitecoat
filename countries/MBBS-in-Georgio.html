About Georgia Overview

MBBS in Georgia

Located in the heart of the Caucasus, Georgia is one of the beautiful countries that offer an ideal environment for international students who want to study MBBS. It is a country located between Eastern Europe and Western Asia, famous for its breathtaking landscapes, rich cultural heritage, and welcoming atmosphere.

With a history that traces back thousands of years, Georgia finds representation in its old landmarks and lively capital: Tbilisi. This is a city of culture with many famous places, including Narikala Fortress, Svetitskhoveli Cathedral, and Vardzia Cave Monastery. To those who come living in Georgia as a medical student, such historical and cultural experiences add immense value.

From the viewpoint of foreign students, Georgia is very attractive due to its relatively low living costs. A moderate climate—pleasantly warm summers and mild winters—adds to the comfort of staying. Equipped with modern infrastructure, efficient public transport, and a good record for safety, the country remains an excellent destination for students.

All Requirements for MBBS in Georgia

Understand the essential criteria and requirements

Ensure your eligibility for pursuing MBBS and starting your medical journey.

Eligibility Criteria to Apply

To secure MBBS admission in Georgia, students must meet the following criteria:

  • Age should be 17 years by 31st December in the Admission Year.
  • Must have completed 10+2 education with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology.
  • A minimum of 50% in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology is required.
  • NEET should be qualified (for Indian students).
  • Clear an Interview/Entrance Exam (Online Mode).

You will need essential documents like 10th & 12th mark sheets, Passport, NEET result, PCC, Bank Statement, and Sponsorship Affidavit.

MBBS Intake in Georgia

The admission process is accessible, but preparing ahead is crucial for a smooth transition.

  • The primary MBBS intake in Georgia generally starts in September.
  • Some universities may also offer a small secondary intake in February or March, depending on seat availability.
  • Students are advised to begin the application process several months in advance, as seats fill quickly and documentation and visa processing take time.

Applying early increases the chances of securing admission to top medical universities in Georgia.

Pathway Particulars

Duration of MBBS Course

The program lasts for 6 years: 5 years of academic training (theory, practicals, labs, clinical subjects) followed by 1 year of clinical internship (hands-on training in hospitals under experienced doctors). Most universities follow international standards preparing students for global exams.

Medium of Teaching

The medium of teaching is primarily English. All lectures, practical classes, laboratory work, and clinical training are conducted in English. Students are also taught basic Georgian during the course to help them communicate with local patients during hospital rotations.

MBBS Admission In Georgia Process

A step-by-step roadmap to admission

  1. Fill out the application form: Complete the admission form from the official RMC website.
  2. Select your University: Choose your desired NMC-approved university to study MBBS in Georgia.
  3. Submit Documents: Be ready with and submit scanned copies of your 10th and 12th mark sheets, NEET scorecard, copy of a medical certificate, and passport.
  4. Receive Invitation: After 10-15 days of applying and submitting the form, you'll receive the invitation letter from the university.
  5. Apply for Visa: Students can apply for a visa at the respective embassy, which usually takes about 10 days for visa processing.

Career Opportunities After MBBS

Graduates have a wide array of pathways available after completing their 6-year medical degree in Georgia.

  • Practice in India: Appear for the FMGE/NExT exam or pursue PG courses (MD/MS).
  • Global Licensing: Prepare for USMLE (USA) or PLAB (UK) to practice internationally.
  • Local Opportunities: Work as a medical professional or researcher in Georgian clinics.
  • Non-Clinical Roles: Medical writing, healthcare management, or public health.
MBBS in Georgia Syllabus

Comprehensive medical curriculum

Students will cover the following theoretical and practical subjects over the duration of the 6-year course in Georgia.

First Year

Human Anatomy, General Psychology, Elective Courses, Bioorganic Chemistry, Internal Medicine, Cytology.

Second Year

Histology, Microbiology, Biochemistry, Anatomy, Physiology, Patient Care, and psychology.

Third & Fourth Year

Year 3: ENT, Embryology, Radiology, Forensic Medicine, Medical Chemistry.
Year 4: Pharmacology, Surgery, Allied Subjects, Community Medicine.

Fifth & Sixth Year

Year 5: Clinical Phase, Gynecology, Infectious Disease, Obstetrics & Ophthalmology, etc.
Year 6: Internship based year under the guidance of a Senior Doctor in Private or Government Hospitals.
