All Requirements for MBBS in France

Understand the essential criteria and requirements

Ensure your eligibility for pursuing MBBS and starting your medical journey.

Eligibility Criteria to Apply

To secure MBBS admission in France, students must meet the following criteria:

  • Age should be 17 years by 31st December in the Admission Year.
  • Must have completed 10+2 education with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology.
  • A minimum of 70% to 75% in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology is required.
  • 10+2 education must have been in an English-medium stream.
  • NEET should be qualified and University Entrance Exam cleared.

Required Documents

Students applying for MBBS in France must submit the following documents:

  • 10th & 12th Mark sheet & Passing Certificate
  • Transfer Certificate & Birth Certificate
  • Passport, Aadhaar copy & NEET result
  • 6 Month Bank Statement & Sponsorship Affidavit
  • PCC, Medical certificate/HIV & 10 Photographs
  • 2 Recommendation Letters, IELTS/TOEFL Result, Statement of Purpose, CV & Letter of Motivation.

Pathway Particulars

Duration of MBBS Course

The MBBS equivalent program in France (General Medicine) takes between 8 to 11 years to complete. This comprehensive duration is divided into three major cycles (PCEM, DCEM, and DES) and includes rigorous specialization and advanced clinical practice.

Language Requirements

While the core medium of teaching can be English, interacting with patients during clinical phases strictly requires French proficiency. Students are expected to have an IELTS 6.0-7.0 or TOEFL 80-100, and a B2 level in French.

Admission Procedure

How to apply to MBBS in France

With all these benefits, knowing how to apply is a key step to undertake for aspiring medical students to achieve a world-class education.

  1. Submit Application: Prepare and submit your admission form along with your Statement of Purpose, CV, and Letter of Motivation.
  2. Document Verification: Provide your 10th and 12th mark sheets, NEET scorecard, Passport, and IELTS/TOEFL/French B2 certificates for university verification.
  3. Entrance Exam/Interview: Clear the university-specific entrance exam or interview process if required.
  4. Receive Offer Letter: Upon successful screening, receive your official admission or offer letter from the French medical institution.
  5. Visa & Financials: Provide your Sponsorship Affidavit, 6-month Bank Statement, and PCC to process your French student visa via Campus France.

Scholarships Offered for MBBS in France

France offers various scholarships to foreign students, which reduce the costs of MBBS in France significantly. Students should confirm eligibility and deadlines while applying.

  • Eiffel Excellence Scholarship: Published by the French Ministry of Europe. Pays tuition, monthly allowance, and traveling expenses.
  • Émile Boutmy Scholarship: Available to non-EU students in some universities. Offers discounts on part-time tuition fees.
  • French Govt Scholarships (Campus France): Includes scholarships, housing support, and allowances.
  • Erasmus+ Scholarship: Assists foreign students enrolled in European institutions.

MBBS Syllabus in France

A comprehensive 3-Cycle Curriculum

Medical education in France is famously rigorous and divided into three distinct cycles. Other courses like Dentistry (6 years) and Pharmacy (5 to 6 years) are also available.

First Cycle (PCEM) - 2 Years

Focus: Fundamental medical sciences and preparatory studies.
Key Subjects: Anatomy, Biophysics, Biochemistry, Physiology, Pharmacology, Epidemiology, Health Economics.

Second Cycle (DCEM) - 4 Years

Focus: Clinical training and hospital internships.
Key Subjects: Pathology, Internal Medicine, Surgery, Pediatrics, Pharmacology, Bacteriology, Virology.

Third Cycle (DES) - 3 to 5 Years

Focus: Specialization and advanced clinical practice.
Key Subjects: Cardiology, Surgery, Psychiatry, Radiology, Research Project.
