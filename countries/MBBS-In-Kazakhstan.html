Eligibility Criteria to Apply

To secure MBBS admission in Kazakhstan, students must meet the following criteria:

  • Age should be 17 years by 31st December in the Admission Year.
  • Must have completed 10+2 education with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology as compulsory subjects.
  • A minimum of 50% in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology is required.
  • NEET should be qualified for Indian students.
  • University Entrance Exam (Online Mode) might be applicable for some institutions.

Required Documents & Intake

The academic year generally begins in September or October. It is highly recommended to apply between June and July to ensure sufficient time for document verification and visa processing.

Documents Needed:

  • 10th Mark sheet & 12th Mark sheet & Passing Certificate.
  • Valid Passport & Aadhaar copy.
  • NEET result scorecard.
  • 10 White Background Photographs with 70% Face (Size 3.5 X 4.5 CM).

Pathway Particulars

Duration & Structure

The total duration is 6 years. The first 5 years focus on theoretical and clinical studies covering pre-clinical, para-clinical, and clinical subjects. The final year consists of a mandatory 12-month rotating internship at university-affiliated hospitals, fully complying with NMC regulations (54 months study + 12 months internship).

Medium of Teaching

The medium of instruction is English for international students. All lectures, exams, and labs are in English. However, universities include mandatory local language classes (Kazakh or Russian) to ensure effective communication with patients during clinical rotations.

MBBS Admission In Kazakhstan

A step-by-step roadmap to admission

The admission process is quite simple, and most universities do not require students to take heavy entrance tests.

  1. Submit Application Form: Complete the university admission application and submit initial documents.
  2. Select your University: Choose an NMC/WHO approved Kazakh medical university.
  3. Document Verification: Provide your 10th/12th mark sheets, valid passport, and NEET scorecard.
  4. Receive Invitation Letter: Upon meeting criteria, receive the official university admission/invitation letter.
  5. Apply for Visa: Proceed with the student visa process and travel arrangements. Apply early (June-July) for the September intake.

Scholarships for MBBS in Kazakhstan

Many universities offer scholarships to international students, making education even more affordable.

  • Merit-Based: Scholarships covering partial or full tuition fees are available based on excellent academic performance in previous education.
  • University-Specific: Institutions like Al-Farabi and Astana Medical University offer their own grants and aids.
  • External Grants: Students may also explore scholarships from international bodies or their home country.

Career Opportunities After MBBS

An MBBS degree from a recognized Kazakh university opens up diverse career pathways globally.

  • Practice in India: Clear the FMGE/NExT exam, complete a 1-year internship in India, and register with the NMC.
  • Global Licensing: Eligible for international exams like USMLE (USA), PLAB (UK), AMC (Australia), and MCCQE (Canada).
  • Postgraduate Studies: Appear for NEET-PG (or NExT-PG) to pursue MD/MS courses in India.
  • Non-Clinical Roles: Opportunities in Healthcare Management (MBA), Public Health (MPH), Medical Tourism, and Research.
