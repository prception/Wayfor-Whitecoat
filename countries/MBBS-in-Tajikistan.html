MBBS in Tajikistan

Studying MBBS in Tajikistan is highly cost-effective compared to private medical colleges in India and many Western countries. Along with low tuition fees, the cost of living in Tajikistan is also affordable, helping students manage their expenses while receiving quality medical education.
