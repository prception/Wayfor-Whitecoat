All Requirements for MBBS in Romania

Understand the essential criteria and requirements

Ensure your eligibility for pursuing MBBS and starting your medical journey.

Eligibility Criteria to Apply

To secure MBBS admission in Romania, students must meet the following criteria:

  • Age should be 17 years by 31st December in the Admission Year.
  • Must have completed 10+2 education with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology.
  • A minimum of 70% to 75% in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology is required.
  • 10+2 education must have been in an English-medium stream.
  • NEET should be qualified and University Entrance Exam cleared.

Required Documents & Intake

The main intake for MBBS starts in September. Applications typically open from May to July. A secondary intake is rarely offered, so students are advised to apply early.

Documents Needed:

  • 10th & 12th Mark sheet & Passing Certificate.
  • Transfer Certificate, Birth Certificate & Passport.
  • Aadhaar copy, NEET result & PCC.
  • Sponsorship Affidavit & 6 Month Bank Statement.
  • 10 Photographs, 2 Recommendation Letters, IELTS/TOEFL Result, SOP, CV & Medical certificate/HIV.

Campus Life & Living Expenses

Cost of Living €300-€500 per month (approx. ₹22,500).

Living in Romania is cheaper compared to most European countries. Romanian universities offer on-campus hostels which cost between €150 to €300 per month. Private apartments vary from €200-€400. Food is pretty affordable (average €100-€150/month for groceries).

Public transport is good and relatively cheap. A monthly bus, tram, and metro ticket costs as low as €10-€20. Campus life is comfortable and diverse, offering modern classrooms, advanced laboratories, safe hostels, and access to Indian food.

Pathway Particulars

Duration & Structure

The MBBS program typically spans 6 years. This includes 3 years of pre-clinical theoretical study followed by 3 years of intensive clinical training in affiliated hospitals, participating in practical sessions and hands-on medical exposure.

Medium of Teaching

The medium of instruction is primarily English, making it suitable for international students. Some universities may offer bilingual programs where students learn basic Romanian to communicate effectively with local patients.

MBBS Admission In Romania

A step-by-step roadmap to admission

The admission procedure is fairly simple and straightforward, designed to accommodate national and international students.

  1. Fill out the application form: Fill out the admission form from the RMC website.
  2. Select your University: Select your NMC-approved desired university for studying MBBS.
  3. Submit Documents: Submit scanned documents - 10th and 12th mark sheets, NEET score card, copy of the medical certificate, and passport.
  4. Receive Invitation: After 10-15 days of applying, you’ll get the invitation letter from the university.
  5. Apply for Visa: Students can apply for a visa in the respective embassy for 10 days of visa processing.

Top Universities in Romania

Romania has universities highly valued for study and research, recognized for academic excellence.

  • Babes-Bolyai University (BBU): The largest university, known for high research output and international cooperation.
  • University of Bucharest: Prestigious institution with international acknowledgment and over 100 international partnerships.
  • Alexandru Ioan Cuza University (UAIC): The oldest university in Romania, established in 1860.
  • Politehnica University of Bucharest: A leading technical university focused on engineering and technology.

After completing MBBS in Romania, Indian students have multiple career options.
