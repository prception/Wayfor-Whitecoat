About Egypt Overview

MBBS in Egypt

Egypt, officially known as the Arab Republic of Egypt, is a transcontinental country located in the northeast corner of Africa, with a small portion extending into Asia. It is bordered by the Mediterranean Sea to the north, Sudan to the south, Libya to the west, and the Red Sea and Israel to the east. Known for its historical significance, Egypt is often referred to as the “cradle of civilization” due to its rich contributions to art, science, and architecture.

Egypt is gifted with numerous world-famous landmarks that include the Pyramids of Giza, the Sphinx, and the longest river in the world, the Nile. Ancient Egyptian civilization founded the root base of modern sciences, mathematics, and medicines, so it also bears importance as a center for historical research. Egypt attracts tourists and scholars alike to its vast archaeological sites and rich cultural heritage, home to treasures preserved from the era of the Pharaohs.

But besides all its historical significance, modern-day Egypt is a country of vibrancy and progress: a steadily developing economy, educational and health infrastructure, and technological advances. Its capital city, Cairo, is a busy metropolis where ancient traditions are alive along with modern culture. Considering the low cost of living, modern medical infrastructure, and cosmopolitan environment, international students come to study MBBS in Egypt in some of its leading medical schools and universities.

All Requirements for MBBS in Egypt

Understand the essential criteria and requirements

Ensure your eligibility for pursuing MBBS and starting your medical journey.

Eligibility Criteria to Apply

To secure MBBS admission in Egypt, students must meet the following criteria:

  • Age should be 17 years by 31st December in the Admission Year.
  • Must have completed 10+2 education with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology as compulsory subjects.
  • Minimum of 60 to 75% aggregate marks in 10th and 12th.
  • There should not be less than 50% marks in any subject.
  • NEET should be qualified.

You will need essential documents like 10th & 12th mark sheets, Birth Certificate, Passport, Aadhaar copy, NEET result, 6 Month Bank Statement, and 15 White Background Photographs with 70% Face.

MBBS Intake in Egypt

The admission process is accessible, but preparing ahead is crucial for a smooth transition.

  • The primary intake for MBBS in Egypt usually begins in September (Fall Intake).
  • Some universities may also offer an additional intake in February or March, depending on seat availability.
  • Students are advised to apply early to secure admission in their preferred university.

MBBS Admission In Egypt Process

A step-by-step roadmap to admission

  1. Fill out the application form: Fill out the application/admission form on the RMC website.
  2. Select your University: Select your NMC-approved desired university for MBBS in Egypt.
  3. Submit Documents: Prepare all necessary documents and submit scanned copies — 10th and 12th mark sheets, NEET scorecard (if available), medical certificate, and passport.
  4. Receive Invitation: Within 10–15 days of submitting the application, you will receive the invitation letter from the university.
  5. Apply for Visa: Apply for a student visa at the respective embassy (visa processing usually takes about 10 days).
