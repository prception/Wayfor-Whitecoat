About Germany Overview

MBBS in Germany

Germany, located in Central Europe, is a developed nation with a long history, advanced technology, and a strong economy. It is the largest economy in Europe and a leading country in innovation, research, and education.

The nation has a high-level healthcare system, which makes it an ideal destination for students interested in studying medicine. Germany provides medical students with state-of-the-art training, resulting in great employment opportunities after graduation.

The living costs are low, with scholarships, part-time jobs, and low-cost accommodation easily accessible. Studying in Germany presents a unique experience with exposure to the best medical education as well as multiculturalism.

All Requirements for MBBS in Germany

Understand the essential criteria and requirements

Ensure your eligibility for pursuing MBBS and starting your medical journey.

Eligibility Criteria to Apply

Students should satisfy the below criteria in order to secure admission:

  • Age should be 17 years by 31st December in the Admission Year.
  • Completed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology (Minimum 70% to 75%).
  • 10+2 education must have been in an English-medium stream.
  • NEET should be qualified.
  • University Entrance Exam is required.

Required Documents & Intake

Admission is very competitive, with a bi-annual cycle: Summer Intake (April-May) and Winter Intake (Sept.-Oct.). Apply 6-12 months prior.

Documents Needed:

  • 10th & 12th Mark sheet & Passing Certificate.
  • Transfer Certificate, Birth Certificate & Passport.
  • Aadhaar copy, NEET result & PCC.
  • Sponsorship Affidavit & 6 Month Bank Statement.
  • IELTS/TOEFL Result, SOP, CV & Recommendation Letters.

Pathway Particulars

Duration & Structure

The program consists of 6 years and 3 months of rigorous training. It includes a Pre-Clinical Phase (2 years), Clinical Phase (3 years), and a Practical Year (1 year) for clinical experience, followed by the State Examination to get a license.

Medium of Teaching

The instruction language is primarily German in state universities. Students must acquire C1-level (or B2) proficiency for admission, as clinical training and patient interactions are conducted in German.

MBBS Admission In Germany

A step-by-step roadmap to admission

Admission is very competitive, so international students should apply 6-12 months prior to the program start date.

  1. Fill out the admission form: Visit our official website and complete the form.
  2. Verification: The university will verify your application form and uploaded documents (passport, transcripts, NEET).
  3. Screening: The university undertakes a screening process to determine eligibility.
  4. Visa Process: Apply for a student visa to the nearest Embassy or Consulate, attaching confirmation of admission.
  5. Enrollment: Receive the physical admission letter, travel to Germany, complete enrollment, and start education.

Career Opportunities After MBBS

Graduation in medical schools in Germany offers excellent professional prospects in the whole world.

  • Practice in Germany: After clearing the Approbation (Medical Licensing Exam), graduates can work as physicians with good remunerations.
  • Practice in India: Degrees are recognized in India; students must clear the FMGE.
  • Specialization: Germany offers great postgraduate (MD/MS) medical programs with research opportunities.
  • Global Practice: German MBBS degrees are recognized globally, allowing students to work in the UK, USA, etc..

MBBS in Germany Syllabus

The systematic approach guarantees excellent education.

  • 1st–2nd Year (Pre-Clinical): Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Biochemistry, Human Anatomy, Physiology, Medical Terminology.
  • 3rd–4th Year (Clinical Foundation): Pathology, Microbiology, Pharmacology, Hygiene, Medical Psychology.
  • 5th–6th Year (Clinical Phase): Internal Medicine, Surgery, Pediatrics, Psychiatry, Neurology, Specialty Rotations.
  • Final Year (Practical Year): Hands-on rotations in hospitals: Internal Medicine, Surgery, Patient Management.
