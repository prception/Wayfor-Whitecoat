All Requirements for MBBS in Armenia

Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible to study MBBS in Armenia, a student needs to satisfy the following:

  • Age should be 17 years by 31st December in Admission Year.
  • Must have completed 10+2 education with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology as compulsory subjects.
  • A minimum of 50% in Physics, Chemistry, and Biology is required.
  • NEET should be qualified.

Required Documents & Intake

The primary intake for MBBS in Armenia begins in September, with some universities offering a secondary intake in February.

Documents Needed:

  • 10th Mark sheet & 12th Mark sheet & Passing Certificate.
  • Passport, Aadhaar copy & NEET result.
  • Sponsorship Affidavit & 6 Month Bank Statement.
  • PCC (Police Clearance Certificate).
  • 10 White Background Photographs with 70% Face (Size 3.5 X 4.5 CM).

Pathway Particulars

Duration of MBBS Course

The duration of the MBBS course in Armenia is typically 6 years. This includes 5 years of academic and theoretical learning followed by 1 year of clinical internship, where students gain practical training in hospitals and medical centers.

Medium of Teaching

The medium of teaching for MBBS in Armenia is mainly English, making it suitable for Indian and other international students. Some universities also provide basic Armenian language classes to help students interact with local patients during clinical training.

MBBS Admission In Armenia

A step-by-step roadmap to admission

The admission process is straightforward. Students do not need to clear exams like IELTS or TOEFL. Admission is granted based on marks in 12th-grade science subjects.

  1. Submit Application Form: Fill out the admission form via the official portal with basic details.
  2. Select your University: Choose an NMC and WHO-approved medical college in Armenia.
  3. Provide Documentation: Submit 10th and 12th-grade marks (showing minimum 50% in PCB) and your valid NEET score.
  4. Receive Invitation: Upon selection based on merit, receive the official university admission letter.
  5. Apply for Visa: Students are advised to apply early as seats fill quickly and the visa process takes time; utilize the Bank Statement and PCC for processing.

Scholarships Offered for MBBS in Armenia

Scholarships are awarded by Armenian universities as a form of financial aid to deserving students. Some notable scholarships include:

  • SIT Zero Fees English Scholarship: Provides tuition fee relief to international students demonstrating good academic performance.
  • Education Future Int. Scholarship 23: Open to Indian students pursuing higher education abroad, helping cover tuition and living expenses.
  • Sir Ratanji Dalal Research Scholarship: Awarded to students who show strong academic merit and research potential.

Career Opportunities After MBBS

Indian students who complete their MBBS in Armenia have a wide range of career opportunities both in India and abroad.

  • Practice in India: Students can return to India and appear for the FMGE/NExT exam to obtain a medical license.
  • Global Licensing: Students can prepare for global exams such as USMLE, PLAB, or AMC.
  • Postgraduate Studies: Pursue MD or MS in India, Armenia, or foreign countries like the UK, USA, Germany, and Australia.
  • Other Domains: Opportunities are available in medical research, public health, healthcare administration, and telemedicine.
MBBS Syllabus in Armenia

Comprehensive medical curriculum

Armenian medical colleges join theoretical knowledge with practical training. Here is a breakdown of the syllabus year by year.

1st & 2nd Year

1st Year: Anatomy, Histology, Medical Biology, Biochemistry, Physiology, Introduction to Medicine.
2nd Year: Pathology, Microbiology, Pharmacology, Immunology, Genetics, Clinical Skills Training.

3rd & 4th Year

3rd Year: General Medicine, Pathophysiology, Diagnostic Techniques, Community Medicine.
4th Year: Internal Medicine, Surgery, Pediatrics, Obstetrics & Gynecology, Radiology.

5th & 6th Year

5th Year: Orthopedics, ENT, Ophthalmology, Emergency Medicine, Psychiatry, Family Medicine.
6th Year (Clinical Internship): Clinical Rotations in Internal Medicine, Surgery, Pediatrics, Gynecology, Emergency Medicine, and other specialties.
